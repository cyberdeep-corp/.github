## 1. Our Commitment

CyberDeep Corporate is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in our projects and communities.

We believe that technological progress is strengthened through collaboration, diverse perspectives, responsible research, and professional conduct.

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of behaviour expected from everyone interacting with CyberDeep Corporate and its associated projects.

It applies regardless of experience, technical background, nationality, age, identity, or organisational affiliation.

## 2. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within CyberDeep Corporate's ecosystem, including:

- Official GitHub repositories and organisation discussions.
- Issues, pull requests, and code reviews.
- Development and collaboration spaces.
- Official community communication channels.
- Project-related events and activities.
- Public interactions where an individual is explicitly representing CyberDeep Corporate.

Project-specific community rules may supplement this document.

## 3. Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to demonstrate professionalism, integrity, and respect.

### Respectful Communication

- Treat everyone with courtesy and consideration.
- Respect differing opinions and technical approaches.
- Provide constructive and useful feedback.
- Criticise ideas and implementations rather than individuals.
- Maintain professional communication during disagreements.

### Collaboration

- Welcome new contributors and help them understand project conventions.
- Share knowledge and technical experience responsibly.
- Recognise the contributions of others.
- Encourage open discussion and constructive problem-solving.
- Respect maintainers' responsibilities and project decisions.

### Technical Responsibility

- Submit genuine and meaningful contributions.
- Respect software licences and intellectual property.
- Avoid introducing malicious, deceptive, or intentionally harmful code.
- Follow repository-specific development and security policies.
- Handle confidential information responsibly.
- Report security concerns through appropriate private channels.

### Integrity and Accountability

- Be honest about your experience, qualifications, and contributions.
- Do not impersonate maintainers or organisational representatives.
- Acknowledge mistakes and take responsibility for your actions.
- Do not misrepresent project status, affiliations, or organisational endorsements.

## 4. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

### Harassment and Discrimination

- Harassment, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Discriminatory remarks or conduct.
- Threats, personal attacks, or deliberate humiliation.
- Unwelcome sexualised comments or attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.

### Community Disruption

- Repeated spam or irrelevant promotional activity.
- Deliberate disruption of technical discussions.
- Trolling or persistent bad-faith participation.
- Abusive behaviour towards contributors or maintainers.
- Deliberate interference with project collaboration.

### Technical Misconduct

- Submitting malicious or intentionally compromised code.
- Attempting unauthorised access to accounts, repositories, or infrastructure.
- Publishing credentials, private keys, or confidential information.
- Misusing organisational resources or privileges.
- Misrepresenting security research authorisation.
- Deliberately introducing vulnerabilities or concealing known security risks.

## 5. Security and Confidentiality

Security and privacy are fundamental responsibilities within the CyberDeep Corporate ecosystem.

Participants must:

- Respect the confidentiality of private information.
- Avoid exposing personal information or credentials.
- Use authorised channels for vulnerability reporting.
- Respect access permissions and organisational boundaries.
- Avoid unauthorised testing of infrastructure and services.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported in accordance with our [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md).

Public disclosure of sensitive vulnerability details without appropriate coordination is discouraged.

## 6. Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers and authorised representatives are responsible for helping maintain a safe and constructive environment.

Their responsibilities include:

- Communicating expectations clearly.
- Reviewing reports of misconduct fairly.
- Addressing violations appropriately.
- Protecting contributors from harassment.
- Maintaining confidentiality during investigations.
- Applying this Code of Conduct consistently.

Maintainer authority should be exercised responsibly and without discrimination or personal retaliation.

## 7. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it through an appropriate official CyberDeep Corporate communication channel.

A report should ideally include:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant dates and times.
- The affected project or communication space.
- Relevant usernames or accounts.
- Supporting evidence, where available.
- Any immediate concerns regarding safety or confidentiality.

Please avoid publicly sharing sensitive personal information or private correspondence.

Reports should be handled with appropriate discretion.

## 8. Enforcement

CyberDeep Corporate reserves the right to take reasonable action in response to violations of this policy.

Depending on the nature and severity of the incident, actions may include:

| Level | Possible Action |
|---|---|
| Minor | Informal warning or clarification |
| Moderate | Formal warning or removal of inappropriate content |
| Serious | Temporary restriction of participation |
| Severe | Removal from project spaces or permanent access restrictions |

The severity, context, frequency, and impact of the conduct will be considered.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and documented where appropriate.

Participants may be given an opportunity to provide relevant context or appeal a decision through an appropriate organisational channel.

## 9. Anti-Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports misconduct in good faith, assists an investigation, or raises a legitimate concern is prohibited.

This includes intimidation, harassment, threats, or deliberate attempts to damage another participant's reputation for making a report.

False or deliberately malicious reports may themselves be subject to review.

## 10. Fairness and Due Process

We aim to handle conduct-related concerns fairly and objectively.

Where reasonably possible:

- Reports will be reviewed by an appropriate authorised individual.
- Relevant evidence will be considered.
- Conflicts of interest should be avoided.
- Confidential information will be handled carefully.
- Actions should be proportionate to the circumstances.

No participant should be subjected to arbitrary enforcement or retaliation.

## 11. Relationship with Other Policies

This Code of Conduct operates alongside other CyberDeep Corporate policies, including:

- `SECURITY.md` — Security reporting and responsible disclosure.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` — Contribution standards and development workflow.
- `SUPPORT.md` — Support and assistance procedures.
- Repository-specific licences and documentation.

Where additional project-specific rules exist, participants should follow those requirements as well.

## 12. Policy Maintenance

CyberDeep Corporate may revise this document as its projects, communities, and organisational practices evolve.

The latest published version is the authoritative version for the organisation's GitHub ecosystem, subject to applicable project-specific policies.

We believe that a strong technology community is built not only through the quality of its software but also through the integrity of the people who create it.

Every contributor, maintainer, and participant has a role in maintaining that standard.
